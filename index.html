📚 The science behind this pairing

Based on research linking personality traits and sensation-seeking to food and sensory preferences.

  • Capsaicin triggers endorphin release, mirroring the adrenaline rush of intense films.
  • High sensation-seekers (action/horror fans) are ~6× more likely to enjoy spicy food.
  • Comfort foods activate nostalgia and emotional resilience pathways — ideal for dramas.
  • Sweet or cooling drinks help reset the palate between plot twists.

Source: Byrnes & Hayes (2015) — Penn State University
